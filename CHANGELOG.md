# Changelog

All notable changes to the versioned template will be recorded in this file.

## [0.1.0] - Unreleased

Initial versioned release.

### Added

- Thin workspace, workflow, shared-reference, and Project-library routing.
- Bounded Understand, Design, Build, Validate, Assess Readiness, Release, and
  Learn contracts.
- An exact-artifact approval-receipt shape for configured human approval
  surfaces.
- Combined Prototype Evidence and Architecture Investigation templates.
- Stage-scoped engineering profiles and focused context-budget checks.
- Migration guidance from unversioned commit `7f500e7`.

### Changed

- Project records now keep stable intent and evidence links while GitHub, CI,
  approval, and deployment systems own changing live state.
- Product and Technical Specifications separate product choice from technical
  feasibility and bind acceptance and proof to exact artifacts.
- Delivery Assessment separates technical options from proportionate delivery
  shape and exact proof planning.
- ADR and Lesson templates name canonical-source updates and human decisions.

### Removed

- Split prototype and architecture-spike templates.
- Repository Build, Validation, Readiness, Release, and Iteration summary
  templates that duplicated canonical delivery systems.
- The monolithic Spec & Design and Assess Delivery workflow paths.
- Retired unversioned paths, without compatibility aliases or pointer files.
