# UI/UX principles

These are reviewed cross-Project preferences for user flows, interfaces,
accessibility, and interaction design. Project-specific experience and behavior
remain in the Product Specification.

## Principles

- Minimize operational effort without hiding important state, consent, or control.
- Every state should present a clear next action or explain why no action is available.
- Every control must earn its presence by serving a user goal or necessary decision.
- Align incentives and feedback with user success. Do not use manipulation or dark patterns.
- Give consequential actions visible feedback and provide a clear recovery path
  when they fail or can be reversed.
- Treat accessibility as behavior, not later polish. Specify and prove the
  applicable keyboard, focus, perception, motion, and assistive-technology
  expectations at the real interaction boundary.
- Preserve interface ownership. Reuse the product's existing component and
  interaction owners, and do not redesign a third-party interface the product
  does not control.
