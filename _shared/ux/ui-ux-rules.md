# UI/UX review rules

Use these questions when drafting or reviewing a Product Specification or prototype.

- What user goal or necessary decision does each control serve?
- Does the control deserve its space, or is there a simpler flow?
- What friction, confusion, or behaviour could this design create?
- In the initial, empty, loading, error, and completed states, is the next action clear?
- Can ambiguity be reduced with a useful default, example, constraint, or progressive disclosure?
- What behaviour does the product encourage, and does that behaviour support the user's intended outcome?

Record material answers in the Product Specification. These questions are reusable guidance, not a substitute for Project-specific intent.
