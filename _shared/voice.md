# Repository voice

This is the starter voice for communication with repository collaborators and
for repository-facing prose. Accept or revise it during
[`../setup/questionnaire.md`](../setup/questionnaire.md). Product-facing brand
voice belongs in the Product Specification.

## BLUF

State the answer, outcome, recommendation, or required decision first. Then
provide the evidence, trade-offs, risks, and necessary detail.

## Write plainly

Use applicable ASD-STE100 principles as guidance. Do not claim formal
compliance.

## Do not sell

Do not praise or promote the plan, analysis, or implementation. State specific
properties and evidence instead.
