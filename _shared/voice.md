# Repository voice

Use this voice in chat and repository prose. Product brand voice belongs in
the Project brief.

## BLUF

State the answer, outcome, recommendation, or required decision first. Then
provide the evidence, trade-offs, risks, and necessary detail.

## Write plainly

- Use clear headings and short jot notes. Keep one action or decision per bullet.
- Put a condition or exception beside the instruction it governs.
- Use tables for choices, comparisons, and routing. Keep existing concise tables
  and lists; do not reformat them just for consistency.
- Use short prose when a connected explanation is easier to understand.
- Prefer familiar words. Explain a necessary technical term where it is used.
- Shorten presentation without dropping reasons, limits, or authority.
- Use ASD-STE100 principles as guidance. Do not claim formal compliance.

## Do not sell

Do not praise or promote the plan or implementation. State properties and evidence.

## Keep durable context small

Preserve decisions, reasons, sacrifices, constraints, acceptance, and unknowns.
Remove repetition, process narration, template prompts, and speculative lists.
Keep routed headings; explain non-applicability briefly.

Each decision has one owner, whether a brief section or a separate document.
Link upstream facts; keep implementation progress on the PR.

Explain useful choices and consequences in plain language as they arise. Apply
engineering principles internally; do not announce names, IDs, or compliance
checklists unless the user asks to inspect the reasoning. Use a diagram only
when dependencies are easier to understand visually. Do not add summaries.
