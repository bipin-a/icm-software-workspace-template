accepted Project or cross-Project decision. It combines framing, findings, and
disposition until repeated use proves that a separate subworkflow is needed.
It does not replace the Product Specification, Technical Specification, or an
earned ADR. Promote it to a Project if it develops an independent outcome,
priority, implementation, release, or ongoing owner.
