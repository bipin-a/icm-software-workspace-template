# Prototype Evidence

Use this optional artifact when a cheap, inspectable prototype can resolve a
specific product or technical uncertainty before implementation. It combines
the approved brief and resulting findings until repeated use earns a separate
prototype subworkflow. For UI-affecting work in Understand, proposed interface
evidence remains mandatory; use this artifact when that prototype needs a
separate frame, findings record, or disposition.
