# Prototype evidence

Use only when an experiment needs a separate frame or findings record. Remove
prompts; link from the brief or PR. Routine previews need no separate artifact.

## Question and scope

The uncertainty, cheapest faithful experiment, effort limit, evidence target,
and stop condition. Link the existing authority for substantial investigation.

## Artifact and data

Exact preview or experiment, reused code/components, safe data, isolation, and
important differences from production. Prototype evidence does not prove integration.

## Findings and limits

Observed result, remaining uncertainty, and affected product/technical decisions.

## Disposition

Discard, revise, or promote through normal implementation and proof. Link the
canonical decisions changed and their review source; store no approval status.
