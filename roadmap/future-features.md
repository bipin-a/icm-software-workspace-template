## Status

- `Idea` — recorded but not evaluated.
- `Watching` — plausible enough to revisit when related work appears.
- `Likely` — expected direction, but not yet an approved Project.
- `Promoted` — moved into a linked Project.
- `Dropped` — intentionally no longer being considered.
